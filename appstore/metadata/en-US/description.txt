Sketchshelf keeps every hand-drawn diagram, sketch and whiteboard you make in one place, on your Mac.

It is built on the open-source Excalidraw editor, so you get the same canvas, tools, shortcuts and hand-drawn look. What Sketchshelf adds is the part a single browser tab can't give you: a library.

A SHELF FOR ALL YOUR DRAWINGS
• Every drawing in a sidebar, sorted by name or by most recently edited
• Switch between drawings in one click. The one you leave is saved first
• Create, rename and delete right from the sidebar
• Collapse the sidebar when you want the whole window for the canvas

SAVED AS YOU DRAW
• Your work is written to disk a moment after you stop drawing, and again whenever you switch away
• No save button and no export step to remember
• Every drawing is a standard .excalidraw file
• Saves are atomic: if your Mac crashes mid-save, the previous version is still there

EMPHASIS INSIDE ONE TEXT BLOCK
• Colour a phrase blue, red, green or orange
• Highlight it, underline it, or draw a box around it
• Break a phrase out onto its own indented line
• Drag the side of a text block to re-wrap it at the same font size

WORKS WITH THE FILES YOU ALREADY HAVE
• Double-click any .excalidraw file in Finder to add it to your library
• Use File → Open to bring in several at once
• Export any drawing as PNG or SVG, or save a copy anywhere on your Mac
• Files stay in the standard format, so they still open in the Excalidraw web editor

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-in
• No analytics, no tracking, no ads
• Your drawings never leave your Mac

And a full dark mode, sidebar included.
