Privacy policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

Sketchshelf does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

Your drawings

Drawings are saved as files in a folder on your Mac (see Help for where). They are never uploaded anywhere. When you export or save a copy, it goes only to the location you choose in the Save dialog.

No accounts, analytics or tracking

There is no sign-in. The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking code, and it does not connect to the internet. The direct download is distributed through Amore, which hosts the file you download; the app itself never contacts Amore or anyone else.

Third parties

No data is shared with third parties, because none is collected.
