Privacy policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

Sketchshelf does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

Your drawings

Drawings are saved as files on your Mac, inside the app's own folder. They are never uploaded anywhere. When you export or save a copy, it goes only to the location you choose in the Save dialog.

No accounts, analytics or tracking

There is no sign-in. The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking code, and it does not connect to the internet.

Third parties

No data is shared with third parties, because none is collected.
