Sketchshelf

Every hand-drawn diagram, sketch and whiteboard you make, on one shelf on your Mac.

SupportPrivacySource

A shelf for all your drawings

Every drawing sits in a sidebar. Switch between them in one click; the one you leave is saved first. Create, rename and delete without leaving the canvas.

Saved as you draw

There is no save button. Your work is written to disk a moment after you stop drawing, as a standard .excalidraw file.

Emphasis inside one text block

Colour, highlight, underline or box a single phrase without splitting your note into pieces.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no network. Your drawings never leave your Mac.

Sketchshelf is built on the open-source Excalidraw editor (MIT licensed) and opens and saves standard .excalidraw files. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Excalidraw.
